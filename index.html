Expedited treatment options apply only to nonpregnant patients 25 or older.

Situations not covered by the calculator

Unsatisfactory cytology with negative, unknown or no HPV: repeat age-based screening in 2–4 months; HPV triage not recommended. Cotest with unsatisfactory cytology and HPV-positive (untyped): repeat cytology in 2–4 months or colposcopy; HPV 16/18-positive: colposcopy. Colposcopy after 2 consecutive unsatisfactory tests. (G.2, H.2)

After hysterectomy: no vaginal screening unless CIN 2+ was diagnosed in the past 25 years. If hysterectomy was the treatment, get 3 consecutive annual HPV-based tests, then test every 3 years until 25 years after treatment. (K.4)

Symptomatic patients (abnormal bleeding, a visibly abnormal cervix) need diagnostic evaluation, not screening algorithms.

Over 65 with prior abnormalities: manage as for ages 25–65. Stopping surveillance is unacceptable if the patient is in reasonably good health. (K.5)

Not yet included: p16/Ki-67 dual-stain triage (2024), self-collected HPV samples (2024) and extended genotyping. See the ASCCP updates.

Decision support only. It does not replace clinical judgment or the full guideline. It covers asymptomatic patients with a cervix.
